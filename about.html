On the island at opening

Everything in Wave 1.

A real little town

A dock where you arrive, a main street of shops, a plaza with the post office and museum, and blocks of homes. Start in a tent and grow it into a house with Shells, the island currency.

Fishing

Cast a line off the pier and see what's biting this season.

Bug catching

Butterflies, beetles and rare finds that only show up at the right time of year.

The museum

Donate your catches and fill every hall together.

Real-time seasons

The island follows the real calendar. Right now it's autumn.

Post office and news board

Send letters and gifts, even to friends who are offline, and keep up with what's happening around town.

Earn Shells

Sell what you catch and spend Shells, the island currency, on your home.

What's coming next

The island opens in waves, so there's always something new.
