If something goes wrong

"You are not whitelisted on this server"

You haven't been added yet, or your name was typed differently. Message a staff member on Discord with your exact username or gamertag, capital letters included.

"Outdated client" or "Outdated server"

Java players need Minecraft {{ site.mc.version }}. Open the launcher, pick Latest release, and try again.

Bedrock says "Unable to connect to world"

Check the port is {{ site.mc.bedrock_port }}, not the default 19132, and that the address has no spaces at the start or end.

The server shows as offline

We're probably restarting or updating. The Discord will say so.

Still stuck?

Ask in the Discord's general chat with a screenshot of the error, and someone will help you out.
