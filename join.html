  1. Get whitelisted

    SnailMail is whitelist only. Join the Discord and message a staff member with your Bedrock gamertag.

  2. Open Minecraft

    Works on phones, tablets, Windows, Xbox, PlayStation and Switch. Go to Play › Servers › Add Server.

  3. Add SnailMail

    Type any name you like, then fill in both boxes. Don't leave the port on its default.

    Server address{{ site.mc.ip }}
    Port{{ site.mc.bedrock_port }}

  4. Join and say hi

    Save it, then tap SnailMail to join. Your name shows up with a dot in front in-game. That's normal: it's how the server tells Bedrock players apart.

On Xbox, PlayStation or Switch?

Consoles hide the Add Server button. The easiest way in is to add SnailMail from the phone or Windows app on the same Microsoft account, or ask in the Discord and we'll help.

If something goes wrong

"You are not whitelisted on this server"

You haven't been added yet, or your name was typed differently. Message a staff member on Discord with your exact username or gamertag, capital letters included.

"Outdated client" or "Outdated server"

Java players need Minecraft {{ site.mc.version }}. Open the launcher, pick Latest release, and try again.

Bedrock says "Unable to connect to world"

Check the port is {{ site.mc.bedrock_port }}, not the default 19132, and that the address has no spaces at the start or end.

The server shows as offline

We're probably restarting or updating. The Discord will say so.

Still stuck?

Ask in the Discord's general chat with a screenshot of the error, and someone will help you out.
