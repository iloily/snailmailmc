On Xbox, PlayStation or Switch?

Consoles hide the Add Server button on the Servers tab. The usual way in is to add SnailMail from the phone or Windows app on the same Microsoft account, or ask in the Discord and we'll help.

Stuck?

Fixes for common problems

"You are not whitelisted on this server"

You haven't been added yet, or your name was typed differently. Message a staff member on Discord with your exact username or gamertag, including capital letters.

"Outdated client" or "Outdated server"

Java players need Minecraft {{ site.mc.version }}. Open the launcher, pick Latest release, and try again.

Bedrock says "Unable to connect to world"

Check the port is {{ site.mc.bedrock_port }}, not the default 19132. Then check the address has no spaces at the start or end.

The server shows as offline

Look at the status at the top of the home page. If it says offline, we're probably restarting or updating, and the Discord will say so.

Still stuck?

Ask in the Discord's general chat with a screenshot of the error. Someone will help you out.
