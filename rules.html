Island rules

Keep it cozy

SnailMail is a cozy place, so let's keep it that way. These are the same rules as in the Discord and in-game.

Be kind

No bullying, harassment, hate speech, slurs or drama. Treat everyone like a neighbor.

Keep it cozy

No NSFW, gore or anything you wouldn't show a younger player. Keep swearing light.

No griefing or stealing

Don't break, take or change anything that isn't yours, on the server or in builds. Ask first.

Play fair

No hacked clients, x-ray, dupes or exploits. Found a bug? Report it in the Discord's bug reports instead of using it.

No spam or ads

No spamming, mass pings or self-promo, and don't advertise other servers.

Use the right channel

Keep chats in their channels and post builds in the Discord's showcase.

Respect privacy

Don't share anyone's personal info, including your own.

Listen to staff

If a staff member asks you to stop, stop. Got a problem with a decision? Message a manager calmly.

If a rule is broken

Breaking the rules can mean a warning, a mute, or losing your spot on the whitelist. To report something, message a staff member on the Discord.
