# Adding a news post

1. On GitHub, open the `_posts` folder and click **Add file › Create new file**.
2. Name it `YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md`, for example `2026-12-01-wave-1-is-here.md`. The date in the name is the post's date.
3. Paste this at the top and fill it in:

```
---
title: Wave 1 is here
category: Wave
description: One or two sentences for the news card.
image: /img/your-screenshot.png
---

Write the post here. Use ## for headings and - for bullet points.
```

- `category` is one of `Update`, `Event` or `Wave` (each gets its own stamp colour).
- `image` is optional. Upload screenshots to the `img` folder first. Wide images (16:9) look best.

4. Commit. The post shows up on the site within a couple of minutes.
